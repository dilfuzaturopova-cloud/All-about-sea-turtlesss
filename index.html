Page 3
Introduction

Meet the Sea Turtles!

Splash! A smooth shell rises above the waves—it's a sea turtle! Sea turtles are reptiles that live in the ocean. They are strong swimmers and travel long distances. Sea turtles have been on Earth since the time of dinosaurs.

🇺🇿 Dengiz toshbaqalari okeanda yashaydigan sudralib yuruvchilardir. Ular dinozavrlar davridan beri bor.
Page 4
Anatomy & Features

What Sea Turtles Are Like

Sea turtles are reptiles, which means they have scales and are cold-blooded. They have large, hard shells to protect their bodies. Instead of feet, they have flippers that help them swim.

🇺🇿 Ularning tangachalari bor, sovuqqonli va oyoq o'rnida suzgichlari (flippers) mavjud.
Page 5
Habitat

Where Sea Turtles Live

Sea turtles live in warm and tropical oceans around the world. They swim in open seas, coral reefs, and coastal waters. Female sea turtles come onto sandy beaches to lay their eggs.

🇺🇿 Dunyo bo'ylab iliq okeanlar va marjon qoyalarida yashashadi. Urg'ochilari tuxum qo'yish uchun qumli sohillarga chiqadi.
Page 6
Life Cycle

From Hatchling to Adult

A sea turtle's life begins as an egg buried in the sand. When the egg hatches, a baby turtle called a hatchling crawls toward the ocean. Many hatchlings travel together for safety. As they grow, they spend most of their lives in the sea.

🇺🇿 Kichik toshbaqalar "hatchling" deb ataladi va tuxumdan chiqib okean tomonga emaklab boradi.
Page 7
Diet

What Sea Turtles Eat

Different sea turtles eat different foods. Some eat seagrass and algae. Others eat jellyfish, crabs, or small sea animals. Their diets depend on the species.

🇺🇿 Ba'zilari dengiz o'tlarini yeydi, ba'zilari esa meduza, qisqichbaqa va kichik dengiz jonivorlarini.
Page 8
Migration

Life in the Ocean

Sea turtles spend most of their lives swimming and searching for food. Some sea turtles migrate thousands of kilometers from their feeding areas to their nesting areas.

🇺🇿 Ular ozuqa va tuxum qo'yish hududlari o'rtasida minglab kilometr masofaga ko'chib o'tadilar (migrate).
Page 9
Movement

How Sea Turtles Move

Sea turtles use their strong front flippers like wings to glide through the water. Their back flippers help them steer. On land, they move slowly and crawl across the sand.

🇺🇿 Oldingi suzgichlari qanotdek sirpanish uchun, orqa suzgichlari esa yo'nalishni boshqarish uchun xizmat qiladi.
Page 10
Protection & Conservation

Keeping Safe

Sea turtles stay safe by using their hard shells for protection. They also hide in reefs or swim away from danger. People can help protect sea turtles by keeping beaches and oceans clean.

🇺🇿 Ular qattiq kosalari bilan himoyalanadi va havfdan suzib ketadi. Biz okean va sohillarni toza tutib yordam beramiz.
Page 11 • Super Power
Amazing Fact

Did You Know?

Sea turtles return to the same beach where they were born. Years later, females swim back to lay their eggs there. Scientists think they use the Earth's magnetic field to find their way!

🇺🇿 Toshbaqalar tug'ilgan sohiliga qaytib keladi! Olimlar fikricha, ular Yerning magnit maydonidan foydalanadi.

Page 14 Reference

GLOSSARY (Atamalar Lug'ati)

Key sea turtle and ocean vocabulary from the presentation

🌿

Algae

🇺🇿 Suvo'tlar

Simple water plants that grow in oceans, rivers, and lakes.

🏊

Flipper

🇺🇿 Suzgich

A flat limb that helps sea turtles swim through the water.

🗺️

Migrate

🇺🇿 Ko'chib o'tish

To travel from one place to another during certain times of the year.

🏖️

Nesting areas

🇺🇿 Tuxum qo'yish hududlari

Safe places where sea turtles lay their eggs in the sand.

🪸

Reef

🇺🇿 Marjon rif

An area in the ocean made of coral where many sea animals live.

🦎

Reptile

🇺🇿 Sudralib yuruvchi

A cold-blooded animal with dry skin or scales.

🐢

Sea turtle

🇺🇿 Dengiz toshbaqasi

A large turtle that lives in the ocean.

🌱

Seagrass

🇺🇿 Dengiz maysasi

A plant that grows underwater and provides food and shelter for sea animals.

🛡️

Shell

🇺🇿 Toshbaqa kosasi

The hard outer covering that protects a turtle's body.
